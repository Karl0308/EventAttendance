02 — The Opportunity

Why this, why now

Manual sign-in sheets don't scale to a full campus of events.

Paper lists are slow, easy to fake, and impossible to report on. Students already carry an RFID ID — we turn that card into an instant, tamper-resistant check-in.

FROM

Sign, count, re-type

Hand-written sheets, manual tallying, hours of encoding after every event — with no live picture of who's in the room.

TO

Tap, verify, done

One tap resolves the student, checks the event window, and updates the dashboard live. Reports export the moment the event closes.

03 — Why This Works for You

The promise, in plain terms

Attendance that's fast to run and easy to read.

Our software turns the phones and scanners you already have into a powerful, reliable attendance station — with a big, clear display anyone can read at a glance.

USE WHAT YOU HAVE

No new equipment to buy

The app runs on the phones, tablets, and scanners already in your school. Nothing to procure, nothing to wait for.

WORKS WITHOUT WI-FI

Keeps going when the internet doesn't

If the connection drops, the app keeps recording. It saves every tap on the device and syncs the moment you reconnect.

PLUG-IN RFID READER

A phone becomes the scanner

We supply a compact RFID reader that connects straight to a phone — tap a student ID and it's counted. No dedicated handheld needed.

05 — The Core Flow

One tap, start to finish

A card tap becomes a verified record in under a second.

Capture happens on the device; validation and truth live on the server.

STEP 1
Read card
Reader emits the card's UID to the scan screen.
STEP 2
Resolve student
UID maps to a student — instantly, even offline.
STEP 3
Validate window
Server checks the event time & grace period.
STEP 4
Record status
Present or Late is written — deduplicated on arrival.
STEP 5
Broadcast live
Dashboards update in real time via SignalR.
Present — within grace Late — after grace Absent — no tap by close Excused — set by organizer
06 — Behind the Scenes

Two flows that keep data honest

Nothing gets lost — and nothing gets counted twice.

OFFLINE SYNC When the venue has no signal, taps wait safely and reconcile on reconnect.
STEP 1
Capture offline
Tap is stamped with a unique device tap ID.
STEP 2
Queue locally
Stored on-device in an encrypted local queue.
STEP 3
Detect network
App watches for connectivity, then flushes.
STEP 4
Batch sync
Whole queue posts to the server at once.
STEP 5
De-duplicate
Server ignores repeats — one record per tap.
SIS IMPORT Student records flow in from your Student Information System — repeatable and safe to re-run.
STEP 1
Upload / connect
CSV file, a DB link, or the SIS API.
STEP 2
Map columns
Match source fields once; saved for next time.
STEP 3
Validate
Rows are checked; errors flagged per row.
STEP 4
Upsert
New students added, existing ones updated.
STEP 5
Report
Inserted / updated / failed counts per batch.

The tapping device

Built for the door, not the desk.

Your existing phone or tablet is the tapping station — same app, Android-first. The scan screen is large, loud, and forgiving.

12 — Trust & Data

Secure, integrated, and yours

Student data stays protected and in sync.

◆
SIS integration

Import students from CSV, a read-only DB link, or the SIS API — mapped once, re-runnable, and idempotent.

◆
Permission-based access

Every action is gated by a fine-grained permission, enforced on the server — not just hidden in the UI.

◆
Full audit trail

Every change is logged with who, what, and when — a complete, tamper-evident record.

◆
School-scoped isolation

Data is filtered by school on every query, so records never cross boundaries.

Encrypted in transit

HTTPS only, HSTS, rate-limited login and tap endpoints, secrets in a vault.

JWT authentication

Short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens, hashed at rest.

Tested for integrity

Automated tests cover deduplication and idempotency — the guarantee that a tap counts once.
